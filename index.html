Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Educational Arcade. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information generated while using our interactive educational platforms.

1. Information We Collect

Our website operates as an open educational arcade. We do not require account registration, personal names, email addresses, or payment details to play any of our games.

2. Game Data and Storage

All gameplay scores, puzzle progress, and educational session choices are processed locally within your browser using standard local web technologies. No personal gameplay profiles are compiled or sold to third parties.

3. Third-Party Links

Our platform contains links to educational games and resource materials. Clicking on external game links may direct you to hosted project pages governed by their respective standard environment policies.

4. Cookies and Web Analytics

We use essential functional cookies strictly to maintain core website operational features and verify search indexing parameters.

By clicking "I Agree", you acknowledge that you have read and understood our Privacy Policy and agree to support our mission towards advancing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals through interactive education.
